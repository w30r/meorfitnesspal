# Initial Concept

A food logging and nutrition tracking app for monitoring daily calorie and macro intake.

# Product Guide

## Vision

A personal nutrition companion that makes calorie tracking effortless, fun, and genuinely insightful — turning everyday food logging into a habit that sticks.

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** A small private group of 3–5 people (the user, family, and close friends)
- **Tone:** Personal, minimal, and data-rich — built for people who want real insights without the noise of commercial fitness apps

## Core Problem

Most calorie tracking apps are cluttered, slow, and feel like a chore. MeorFitnessPal exists to remove friction and make logging so rewarding that users *want* to keep coming back.

## Key Principles

1. **Minimal but Insightful** — Clean, uncluttered UI surfaces meaningful data at a glance. Every element earns its place.
2. **Frictionless Logging** — AI-powered food parsing, quick-add favorites, and intuitive date navigation keep the time-to-log under 10 seconds.
3. **Habit-Driven Design** — Streaks, visual progress, and personalized recommendations (TDEE estimates, macro breakdowns) reward consistency.
4. **Privacy-First** — Small, private user base. No ads, no social noise, no data sold.
5. **Data Density** — Charts, averages, comparisons, and trends provide a rich picture of nutrition without overwhelming the user.

## Key Features (Current)

- Meal logging with per-100g nutritional breakdowns
- Daily calorie/macro progress against goals
- Weight tracking with weekly averages and trends
- Streak tracking for logging consistency
- Goal presets (balanced, high-protein, performance)
- AI food parsing via Gemini
- Dashboard with energy balance, macros, weight trends, and streak display

## Future Direction (User Goals)

Make calorie logging more interesting and insightful — driving higher engagement and consistency through better data visualization, gamification elements, and personalized feedback.
